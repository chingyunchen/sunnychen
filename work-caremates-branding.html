Caremates Branding

CareMates is a digital companion for care providers — designed to make admissions, scheduling and oversight feel effortless. The identity had to feel warm enough to hold a hand, and technical enough to run a clinic

The mark resolves around a single idea: care is a continuous loop. A heart folds into an infinity, the checkmark hides inside it — connection and confirmation in one gesture
