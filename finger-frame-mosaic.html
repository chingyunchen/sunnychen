Finger Frame Mosaic

Framing the world with your hands — pinch a rectangle in the air and the camera composes a living mosaic from whatever falls inside it

Type
AI Project
Role
Concept, design & build
Time
2026

Live https://lootai.net/work/r4y6z5mpl8553otu

The idea

People frame things with their hands instinctively — thumbs and index fingers pinched into a rectangle to picture a shot before taking it. Finger Frame Mosaic takes that gesture literally: whatever falls inside the frame you make with your hands is captured and tiled into a growing mosaic.

Because the input is a gesture rather than a button, the piece rewards playfulness. Move closer and the tiles enlarge; sweep across the room and the mosaic fills with fragments of everything you passed.
